Common questions

I lost my progress.
Progress is saved on the device it was earned on. Deleting the app, or clearing website data if you play in a browser, removes it. There is no way to recover a profile once it's gone.
I forgot my password.
Passwords can't be reset, because no account information ever leaves your device — there's no server holding it. You can start a new profile from the sign-in screen.
Can I move my progress to another phone?
Not at present. Profiles are tied to the device.
The leaderboard only shows me.
Unless a shared board has been set up, scores stay on your own device, so you'll be the only name there.
A swipe wasn't accepted.
Curves are judged on direction rather than accuracy — go the right way round and roughly far enough. For shapes with corners, don't lift your finger until the whole shape is traced.
Can I turn off the sound or the motion?
Yes. The speaker button on the play screen mutes audio. If your device has "Reduce Motion" switched on, the background animations stop automatically.
Does it work without internet?
Yes. The whole game is stored on your device and needs no connection.
