SQUOOS Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9/24/2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SQUOOS (“the App”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use SQUOOS, we may collect:

  • Account information: If you create an account, we collect your email address and use authentication information to let you sign up, sign in, and manage your account.
  • Password information: SQUOOS uses Supabase Auth for email-and-password authentication. Supabase Auth stores password hashes rather than plain-text passwords. We do not intend to store your password in readable form.
  • Game information: Your unlocked Squoos, Skins, Reactions, Achievements, and Stats are recorded in your profile to be used in our Leaderboard.

You are able to play without creating an account. If you do, account information will not be collected through account registration, though other information described in this policy may still be processed.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage accounts and authenticate users.
  • Provide account-related features and, if applicable, save and restore game progress.
  • Operate, maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the App.
  • Respond to support requests and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Supabase and Service Providers

SQUOOS uses Supabase to provide account authentication and related backend services. When you create an account or sign in, information such as your email address and authentication data is processed by Supabase to provide those services.

Supabase describes its password-based authentication system as storing salted password hashes rather than plain-text passwords. For more information, see Supabase’s Password Security documentation .

No other third-party services or SDKs are used by SQUOOS to process information.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may provide information to service providers, such as Supabase, when needed to operate the App and its features. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of users or the App.

5. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the App’s features and for legitimate operational, security, and legal purposes.

Account deletion: If you choose to delete your account, it will be entirely removed from Supabase. You will be allowed to sign up again with the email address you had previously used for the deleted account.

6. Security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children’s Privacy

The app is suitable for all ages. Because nothing is transmitted or collected besides an email address and password, no personal information about children is gathered, shared or disclosed to anyone.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning your personal information, such as requesting access, correction, or deletion. To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will update the effective date above and provide any additional notice required by law.
