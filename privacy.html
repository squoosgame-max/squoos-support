SQUOOS Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026 · Rubenstein Games

This policy explains what information the SQUOOS game ("SQUOOS", "the app", "we") collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. We've tried to keep it short and plain.

The short version
  • You can play SQUOOS without an account. Guest progress stays on your device.
  • If you create an account, we store your email address, your game progress, and any leaderboard name and scores you post.
  • SQUOOS shows optional video ads from Google AdMob. They're set up for children: non-personalized, rated G, and with no cross-app tracking.
  • The one-time Remove Ads purchase is handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • We don't sell your information. You can delete your account from inside the app at any time.

1. Information we collect

If you play as a guest

Your progress, settings and scores are saved only on your device. We don't receive them. Leaderboards need an account to post.

If you create an account

InformationWhy we use it
Email addressTo create your account, let you sign in, confirm your address, and reset your password.
PasswordTo sign you in. It's stored securely (hashed) by our account provider; we can't read it.
Profile nameShown in the game. By default it's the part of your email address before the "@", and you can change it.
Game progress and settingsLevel, experience, unlocked characters and skins, achievements, sound and vibration settings, so your progress is saved to your account.
Leaderboard name, 4-digit PIN, scores, level and characterTo post your scores to the shared leaderboards. Your leaderboard name, score, level and character are visible to other players. Your email address is never shown.

Information kept on your device

The app keeps some information on your device only: your progress, whether you've bought Remove Ads (so the game stays ad-free offline), and your email address if you tick "Remember Email" on the sign-in screen.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

SQUOOS offers optional rewarded videos. For example, you can watch a short video to get an extra heart, extra time, or another try in the Daily Challenge. Ads are never required to play.

These ads are provided by Google AdMob. Every ad request from SQUOOS is marked as directed to children (under the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA) and as coming from a user under the age of consent (for European privacy law). SQUOOS also limits ads to a G rating and requests non-personalized ads only. SQUOOS does not ask for permission to track you and does not use the device's advertising identifier for tracking.

To show an ad, count views, cap how often ads appear and prevent fraud, Google's software may still process limited technical information. This can include your IP address, device type, operating system, app version, and how you interacted with the ad. Google handles this under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

The optional Remove Ads purchase ($0.99, one time) is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your name, payment card or Apple ID details. The app only learns whether Remove Ads has been bought, so it can turn ads off and let you restore the purchase on a new device.

What we don't collect

SQUOOS doesn't collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data. It doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

2. How we use information

  • To run the game, save your progress and show leaderboards.
  • To manage your account, including confirmation and password-reset emails.
  • To show optional rewarded ads and provide the rewards you choose.
  • To turn ads off and restore your purchase if you buy Remove Ads.
  • To keep the game working, secure and fair.

We don't sell your personal information. We don't use it for targeted advertising, and we don't send marketing emails.

3. Who we share it with

We only share information with the service providers that make SQUOOS work:

  • Supabase: stores accounts, profiles and leaderboard scores for us.
  • Google AdMob: provides the optional ads, as described above.
  • Apple: processes the Remove Ads purchase and distributes the app.

Leaderboard names, scores, levels and characters are visible to other SQUOOS players. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

4. Children

SQUOOS is designed to be suitable for players of all ages, including students.

  • Children can play without an account. In guest mode, we don't collect personal information from them.
  • Creating an account requires an email address. Accounts are intended for players 13 and older, or younger players whose parent or guardian has given permission.
  • All ads in SQUOOS are requested as child-directed, non-personalized and G-rated, for every player.

If you're a parent or guardian and believe your child created an account without your permission, contact us (see below). We'll delete the account and its information.

5. Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your account information for as long as your account exists.

Delete your account in the app: sign in, open the menu, and tap Delete profile. This permanently deletes your account, your saved profile and progress, and your leaderboard scores from our servers. It also clears them from your device. You can also ask us to delete your account by email.

Guest progress stays on your device until you delete the app.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Play without an account. Nothing about you is stored on our servers.
  • Skip ads. Rewarded videos are always optional. You can decline them, or buy Remove Ads to get the same rewards without videos.
  • Change your leaderboard name from the Leaderboards screen.
  • See, correct or delete your information. Use the in-app options or contact us. Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU/UK), you may have additional rights, and we'll honor them.

7. Security

Account data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with our provider, Supabase. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we'll also let players know in the app or on the App Store listing.
