Privacy policy · last updated 15 September 2026

SQUOOS does not collect, transmit or sell any personal information.

There are no adverts, no analytics, no trackers and no third-party services of any kind in this app.

What the app stores

To remember your progress, the app saves the following on your own device only:

  • your display name and, if you create a profile, an email address and a password (stored as a one-way hash, never as readable text)
  • your scores, level, achievements and unlocked characters
  • your daily challenge history and streak
  • your settings, such as sound and vibration

None of this is sent anywhere. It stays in the app's local storage on the device. Deleting the app deletes all of it permanently.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because nothing is transmitted or collected, no personal information about children is gathered, shared or disclosed to anyone.

Network access

The app plays entirely offline and does not require an internet connection.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
